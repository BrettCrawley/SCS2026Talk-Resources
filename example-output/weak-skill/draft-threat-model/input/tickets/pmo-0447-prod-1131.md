## PMO-0447 — Workplace Productivity Programme, FY26 H2

**Type:** Portfolio item
**Status:** In Delivery
**Portfolio owner:** COO office
**Budget line:** OPEX, Workplace Technology
**Child initiatives:** PROD-1120, PROD-1131, PROD-1142

### Objective

Reduce time lost to tool switching and manual information gathering across engineering and customer-facing functions. Board commitment made in the FY26 planning cycle to demonstrate measurable productivity improvement from AI tooling before the FY27 budget round.

### Success measures

| Measure | Baseline | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Mean time to answer an internal information request | 22 minutes | Under 5 minutes |
| Engineering time spent on status gathering | 6.5 hrs/week/person | Under 3 hrs |
| Adoption across pilot functions | 0% | 60% weekly active |

### Constraints recorded at portfolio level

- Delivery inside FY26 H2. The board reporting date is fixed.
- No net new headcount. Delivery from existing team capacity.
- Running cost must be predictable and attributable to a cost centre.
- Must use approved vendors. Procurement will not run a new vendor assessment inside the delivery window.

### Risks recorded at portfolio level

| ID | Risk | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR-01 | Delivery date is board-committed and immovable | COO office | Accepted |
| PR-02 | Running cost scales with adoption, which is the success measure | Finance | Mitigating via PLAT-2822 |
| PR-03 | AI tooling governance is not yet defined at group level | Legal | Open, workstream not started |

**Note against PR-03:** the group AI governance workstream has not begun. Initiatives are proceeding on the basis that governance will be retrofitted. Reviewed at the July steering group and accepted.

## PROD-1131 — Initiative: Conversational access to internal systems

**Type:** Product initiative
**Parent:** PMO-0447
**Status:** In Progress
**Product owner:** Dana Whitfield
**Engineering owner:** Marcus Oyelaran
**Child epics:** PLAT-2810, PLAT-2790 (search indexing, delivered), PLAT-2856 (analytics, not started)

### Problem

People know the answer exists somewhere. They do not know which system holds it. The cost is not the lookup, it is the context switch and the reconstruction of what they were doing before it.

Support interviews and Platform time-tracking both point at the same shape: the questions are simple, the answers are scattered, and the gathering is manual.

### Approach

An assistant reachable from where people already are, able to read across the systems the organisation runs and act on what it finds, so a request becomes a sentence rather than a sequence of tabs.

Phase 1, this epic: read and act, human present, pilot functions only.
Phase 2, PLAT-2856: usage analytics and expansion.
Phase 3, not yet scoped: unattended operation, customer-facing surfaces.

### Decisions taken at initiative level

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Build on the existing model provider agreement rather than assess a new vendor | Procurement window closed, see PMO-0447 constraints | 2026-03-11 |
| D-02 | Use vendor-supplied MCP connectors rather than build integrations | Faster, and maintenance sits with the vendor | 2026-03-24 |
| D-03 | Assistant acts as the requesting user, not as a system identity | Preserves attribution and existing permission boundaries | 2026-04-02 |
| D-04 | Pilot with Platform and Support | Highest measured time loss, and both were willing | 2026-04-02 |
| D-05 | No new data stores. Read from systems of record in place | Avoids a data duplication and retention problem | 2026-04-15 |

### Open at initiative level

- Group AI governance dependency, PR-03. Unresolved.
- Whether Phase 3 unattended operation is in FY27 scope. Not decided.
- Cost attribution model for shared usage across teams.

### Dependencies

| On | For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Identity Platform team | Delegated authorisation flows | Requested, not scheduled |
| Data Platform team | Model provider account and key management | Provided |
| Security | Review before pilot expansion | Scheduled |
